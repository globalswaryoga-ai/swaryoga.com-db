# QR WhatsApp Backup - Email Guide

## 📧 Which Emails Do I Need?

There are **2 different emails** involved in the backup system. Here's what each one does:

---

## Email #1: Your WhatsApp Backup Account Email
**Purpose:** Sign in to QR WhatsApp backup system

### How to use:
1. Go to QR WhatsApp page
2. Look for "Connect Google Drive" button
3. A setup guide appears with Step 1: "Create Account"
4. Enter **any email address** you want (can be personal, work, or Swar Yoga email)
5. Create a password
6. This email/password logs you into the backup system

### Example:
- Email: `john@gmail.com` or `user@swaryoga.com`
- Password: Your secret password
- **Used for:** Logging into the backup dashboard

### ⚠️ Important:
- This email is **NOT** your Google account email
- This is just for logging into QR WhatsApp backup
- You can use any email address

---

## Email #2: Your Google Account Email
**Purpose:** Connect Google Drive for backup storage

### How to use:
1. After creating account with Email #1
2. Click "Connect Google Drive" button
3. Step 2 & 3 of guide opens Google OAuth
4. You'll be asked to authorize your **Google Account**
5. This Google account's Drive gets the backups

### Example:
- Google Email: `your-google-account@gmail.com`
- This is the email where your Google Drive backup folder appears
- Path: `My Drive > Swar Yoga Backup > WhatsApp > [YourPhone]`

### ⚠️ Important:
- Must be an **actual Google account** (gmail.com or workspace)
- This is where your backups are stored
- Only you can access this Drive
- Can be different from Email #1

---

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Using Personal Gmail
```
Email #1 (Backup Account):  john@gmail.com
Email #2 (Google Drive):    john@gmail.com (same)
```
✅ Simple! Both emails are the same.

### Scenario 2: Using Work Email
```
Email #1 (Backup Account):  john@swaryoga.com
Email #2 (Google Drive):    john@gmail.com (personal)
```
✅ Separate emails - backup stored in personal Google Drive

### Scenario 3: Using Company Google Workspace
```
Email #1 (Backup Account):  john@swaryoga.com
Email #2 (Google Drive):    john@swaryoga.com (workspace)
```
✅ Company Gmail - backup stored in company Google Drive

## Contacts Sync Email

**Good news:** Contacts use the **same Google account** (Email #2)

### What happens:
1. Contacts from your Google Contacts are imported
2. Synced into WhatsApp chat contacts
3. Associated with the Google account you connected (Email #2)

### How to sync:
1. Make sure Email #2 Google account is connected
2. Go to Settings tab → Look for "Google Contacts Sync"
3. Click "Sync Google Contacts Now"
4. Your Google Contacts are imported automatically

## Troubleshooting

### "Invalid email during signup"
- ✅ Make sure Email #1 is a valid format: `name@domain.com`
- ✅ Don't use special characters
- ✅ Can't already be registered

### "Google authentication failed"
- ✅ Email #2 must be an active Google account
- ✅ Go to `myaccount.google.com` to check
- ✅ Make sure 2-factor authentication is enabled for security

### "Can't find Google Contacts after sync"
- ✅ Contacts must be in Google Contacts app
- ✅ Check `contacts.google.com` to see your contacts
- ✅ Click "Sync Google Contacts Now" again

### "Backup shows wrong Google email"
- ✅ Check which email you authorized in Google step
- ✅ That email's Drive gets the backups
- ✅ Email #1 (backup account) doesn't matter for storage
